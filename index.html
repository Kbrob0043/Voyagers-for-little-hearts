The cause behind our campaign

Every breakthrough begins with research.

Congenital heart defects (CHDs) are structural differences in the heart present at birth. The Children's Heart Foundation supports research aimed at improving the diagnosis, treatment, and prevention of CHDs. We believe that mission is worth rallying around.

🫀

Why little hearts matter

Children born with heart defects and their families can face complex medical journeys. Awareness helps more people understand the importance of CHD research.

🔬

Why research matters

Research can help clinicians better understand congenital heart defects and develop improved approaches to care.

🤝

Why we come together

Our cohort is using the power of community, generosity, and collaboration to support a cause bigger than any one of us.

What the Voyagers are doing

Turning generosity into opportunity.

The Voyagers cohort is organizing a charity fundraising and auction initiative benefiting The Children's Heart Foundation. We're reaching out to businesses and community members who may be willing to contribute auction items, services, experiences, or other forms of support.

Every contribution helps us bring people together around the Foundation's research mission. Details about the auction and participation will be shared as they are confirmed.
